# External benchmarks that the report archives and grades along with its own (version 1.3)

Dated predictions by others, with source, target year and disclosed self-interest. No row is ever deleted; the status is updated with every edition.

## WAAI-2026-V01: Geoffrey Hinton, 2016 (target year 2021)

Prediction: Training radiologists should be stopped, because machine learning would read images better than humans within five years.

Self-interest: Researcher, no commercial interest

Status: Missed. Ten years later, the official French statistics count more radiologists, more assistants and more imaging centers than in 2014, with bottlenecks in recruitment.

Sources: [77]

## WAAI-2026-V02: Dario Amodei, Anthropic, May 2025 (target year 2030)

Prediction: Up to half of all entry level positions could disappear within five years.

Self-interest: Vendor whose business depends on the effectiveness of the technology

Status: Qualified by the source himself; reinterpreted in 2026 as a management decision. No measurement criterion named; the report tests against the hiring rate of 22 to 25 year olds in exposed occupations. First interim status: the unemployment rate of more than 10 percent announced alongside it has not materialized, it stood at 4.1 percent in August 2026, while hiring of 22 to 25 year olds in the most replaceable occupations has fallen by roughly a third since 2021.[136][138]

Sources: [27]

## WAAI-2026-V03: AI Futures Project, AI 2027, April 2025 (target year 2027)

Prediction: A system that completes every programming task faster and more cheaply than the best human developers, in the course of 2027; a near complete hiring freeze for programmers from mid 2027.

Self-interest: Authors from safety research, not a vendor

Status: Pushed back by the authors themselves; the median now lies around 2030. The scenario's labor market claims have no evidence base of their own.

Sources: [127]

## WAAI-2026-V04: Gartner, October 2024 (target year 2026)

Prediction: Through 2026, 20 percent of organizations will use AI to flatten their organizational structure, eliminating more than half of current middle management positions.

Self-interest: Market researcher that sells advisory services on the restructuring it describes

Status: Open until year-end, no measurement method stated. Benchmark: Korn Ferry finds in 2025 that 41 percent of respondents report a reduction of management layers, without attributing it to AI; the two documented cases, Block and Meta, attribute the cuts to the technology themselves, and Meta has partly reversed its plan. To be graded in the 2027 edition.

Sources: [205]

## WAAI-2026-V07: Zach Lloyd, Warp, July 2026 (target year 2027)

Prediction: Within a year every significant software project runs an automated development engine; the share of automatically submitted code changes rises from around 20 to between 30 and 60 percent.

Self-interest: Vendor of the tool described

Status: Open. It will be tested against vendor statistics on automatically submitted code changes, with the caveat that these measure output and not value.

## WAAI-2026-V08: Goldman Sachs, 2026 (target year 2028)

Prediction: With full diffusion of today's use cases, 2.5 percent of American employees are affected, the unemployment rate sits half a percentage point above trend during the transition phase and returns to it within two years.

Self-interest: Investment bank with exposure to the technology industry

Status: Open. So far the measured effect lies below this; the benchmark serves as an upper bound for the first force.

## WAAI-2026-V09: Anthropic Institute, September 2026 (target year 2030)

Prediction: In the moderate and the substantial scenario, the American unemployment rate stands at 3.9 and 4.6 percent respectively at the beginning of 2030; only in the extreme scenario does it reach 17.9 percent in office and knowledge occupations.

Self-interest: Research unit of a vendor

Status: Not a prediction but a model calculation; the report carries it as a frame against which the job killer story can be measured, and in 2027 it tests the underlying assumptions about adoption.
