# Work After AI 2026

**The annual assessment of how artificial intelligence is transforming work.** Machine edition of the report *Work After AI 2026: The Great Misconception About the AI Job Killer* by Gerhard Kürner, version 1.3 (Q4 Edition) of November 6, 2026, in English and German.

This repository holds the report in the form that language models and agents read: the full text as Markdown with stable identifiers, the key findings, predictions, external benchmarks and all 205 sources as JSON, and a ready-made skill for agents.

## The eleven key findings in one line each

1. The job killer is a capital markets story.
2. Expectation outruns measurement.
3. The career gap arises in hiring, not in layoffs.
4. The entry level is shifting from execution to oversight.
5. Women carry the higher risk and have the lesser access.
6. What the individual gains does not arrive in the organization.
7. In 2026 the human becomes the bottleneck.
8. Delegating is a leadership decision, and whoever stops checking loses the skill.
9. The organization of the future orders its work by topic instead of by function, and it becomes smaller.
10. Marketing is the first function in which the rebuild becomes visible.
11. In Europe, demographic pressure reverses the opening question.

Full wording with figures and sources: [`en/key-findings.json`](en/key-findings.json).
